Questions

The honest answers

Is my email really private?

Yes. Your mail syncs from Gmail into a local store on your Mac, and every bit of AI runs on-device. No email content or AI inference ever touches a third-party server.

So is it truly "offline"?

All the AI is. Fetching and sending mail naturally has to reach Gmail — that's where your mail lives — but that's the only network call, and it's to your own account. No cloud AI, no telemetry, no brokers.

Why pay if it's open source?

The source is free under AGPL-3.0 — build it yourself anytime. The one-time license buys the signed, notarized, auto-updating build (no Xcode), support, and it funds full-time development. Same model as SQLite, Plausible, and Immich.

Which Macs are supported?

macOS 14+ on Apple Silicon (M1 or newer). 8 GB runs the default small Gemma; 16 GB+ unlocks larger models in the in-app picker.

Gmail only?

At launch, yes. Other providers are on the roadmap.
